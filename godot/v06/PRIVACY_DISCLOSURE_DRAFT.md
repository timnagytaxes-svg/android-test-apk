# Ironbound Chronicles — Privacy disclosure draft

**Scope:** Android offline pre-release game. Review before publishing as a privacy policy.

The current app is designed for offline gameplay. It does not require an account, does not ask for a user's name or email, and its Android export preset disables the Internet permission. No analytics, advertising SDKs, remote logins or purchase tracking have been added to the source repository.

Gameplay saves contain in-game state (quests, battles, levels, equipment, gold and user-selected settings) on the device in Godot's local user-data directory. They are not intentionally sent to a server by the game. The player can remove local data through Android's app-storage controls or uninstall the app.

If any analytics, support services, cloud saves, advertisements, purchases, account systems, or connected features are added, this statement must be updated **before release**.

This draft requires a publisher contact method, effective date, jurisdiction-appropriate disclosures, and independent verification of the final APK and third-party dependencies. This is not legal advice.
