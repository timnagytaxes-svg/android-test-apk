# Ironbound Chronicles — Store listing draft

**Product name:** Ironbound Chronicles  
**Platform:** Android (Godot 4)  
**Genre:** Offline single-player fantasy tactical JRPG  
**Suggested marketing tagline:** Three realms. Four heroes. One last dawn.

## Short description
Explore three fantasy realms, lead a four-hero party, defeat powerful bosses and reclaim the stars in a turn-based offline RPG.

## Description
The world of Ironbound stands at the edge of a long night. As the old Starforger seals begin to fail, four unlikely companions must cross the green hills of Hearthwood, the frozen marshes of Glimmerfen and the twilight heights of Skyvault to restore the dawn.

**Battle with purpose.** Take command of your Warden in tactical turn-based encounters while three independent companions protect, cast and heal. Select your targets, learn your class skills and defeat enemies that react to your actions.

**Grow your party.** Earn experience, discover equipment, enhance weapons and armor, bond with your companions and choose a combat style that suits you.

**Explore and complete the story.** Travel between three connected regions, meet quest characters, discover treasure, defeat sentinels and confront three chapter bosses. Repeatable bounty trials offer extra challenges and rewards.

**Adventure on your own terms.** Play offline, keep progress in three local save slots, choose your difficulty, and toggle sound or reduced motion.
